# CoPilotStudio
The Pham Automation team implemented the Ford Agent at Meadowvale Ford that has revolutionized their invoice processing operations. By leveraging AI automation and integrated solutions like Microsoft Copilot Studio, AI Builder, Power Automate, and SharePoint. 

The Meadowvale Ford Team has achieved remarkable efficiency gains, reducing processing time by up to 80% by eliminating manual process such as monitoring Emails, data entry and scanning.

This transformation has not only streamlined their workflow but also minimized errors and improved accuracy, allowing the team to focus on strategic tasks that enhance overall productivity and customer satisfaction.
